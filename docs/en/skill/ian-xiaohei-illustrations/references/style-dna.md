# Style DNA

## One line

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, white space, restrained, absurd, product-sketch feel, handwritten feel, clear structure but not an instruction manual.

Like an explanatory sketch dashed off on blank paper by someone who has spent years building AI, product, design, and developer tools.

## Must

- 16:9 landscape in-article illustrations.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradients, shadows, noise, or vintage-paper feel.
- Black hand-drawn line art as the base: thin lines, slightly wobbly, not mechanical, not vector, no heavy outlines.
- Lots of white space: subject fills roughly 40%-60% of the frame, at least 35% empty, ideally with one whole quiet zone.
- A few handwritten annotations (in the article's language): at most 5-8, each ideally 1-4 words.
- One image covers exactly one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Express structure naturally; never write the structure type name on the image.

## Color

- Black: main line art, character, frames, structure, primary text, key objects.
- Red: key annotations, problems, emotional beats, critical reminders, results.
- Orange: main flow, paths, arrows, automation direction, movement from A to B.
- Blue: supplementary notes, in-head states, system states, second-layer explanations, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required in every image. Keep color restrained — less is better than more.

## Never

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographics.
- No formal flowcharts.
- No course slides.
- No cute cartoon posters.
- No children's book illustration.
- No complex architecture diagrams.
- No polished flat illustration.
- No techy UI.
- No real app screenshots.
- No busy backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- No explaining every single node.
- No type titles in the top-left like "Workflow flowchart / system architecture / common pitfalls / roadmap".

## Aesthetic direction

Go absurd, creative, interesting, clean and simple, wildly imaginative.

Not cute, childish, overcomplicated, or stiff.

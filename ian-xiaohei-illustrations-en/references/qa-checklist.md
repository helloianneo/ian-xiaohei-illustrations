# QA Checklist

## Must Pass

- 16:9 horizontal image.
- Clean pure white background.
- Xiaohei is present.
- Xiaohei performs the core action and is not decoration.
- The metaphor is fresh for the current article, not a copied example.
- The image is strange, creative, and memorable.
- The composition is clean; the main subject uses no more than about 60% of the canvas.
- One image communicates one core structure.
- English labels are short, sparse, and readable.
- Orange is used only for main paths or arrows.
- Red is used only for emphasis, problems, warnings, or results.
- Blue is used only for secondary notes, feedback, or system state.

## Failure Signals

- A top-left title appears, such as "workflow", "system diagram", "roadmap", or "common traps".
- Xiaohei feels like a mascot, sticker, emoji, or cute cartoon.
- The image looks like a PPT slide, course page, formal flowchart, or architecture diagram.
- Too many nodes, arrows, labels, or explanatory paragraphs.
- The background has paper texture, shadow, gradient, beige tint, or noise.
- It contains realistic UI screenshots or glossy tech-interface styling.
- English text is misspelled or unreadable.
- The visual metaphor is too ordinary or too stiff.
- The composition is too similar to an example image.

## Iteration Rules

- Too ordinary: make Xiaohei the action subject and add a strange but legible physical metaphor.
- Too complex: remove nodes and keep one action with 3-5 short labels.
- Too cute: emphasize deadpan, blank serious expression, not cute, not mascot.
- Too PPT-like: remove title, frame, tidy grid, and extra arrows; turn it into a hand-drawn scene.
- Too similar to examples: keep the core meaning but change the main object and Xiaohei action.
- Bad text: edit locally if minor; regenerate with fewer labels if text is badly wrong.

High-quality images should feel a little strange first, then become understandable within one second.
